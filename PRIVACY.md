# That's Me! – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

That's Me! is a spelling game that helps young children learn to spell and say their own name. It does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

- No account or sign-in is required.
- No analytics, advertising, or tracking tools are used.
- The child's name and the app's settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else.
- The app does not use the camera or microphone and does not connect to the internet.
- Letters and names are spoken using the voice built into your device.
- Deleting the app deletes everything it saved.
